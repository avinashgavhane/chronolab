Why we built an open-source, deterministic diagnostic intelligence engine to solve fragmented patient records.

The Core Dilemma: Healthcare's "Static Snapshot Trap"

When a patient is tested for a routine check-up or manages a chronic illness (such as Type 2 Diabetes, Hypertension, or Thyroid conditions), their diagnostic journey is split across multiple independent laboratories over years:

The Laboratory Reality

Every laboratory utilizes different nomenclature (e.g. FBS vs Glucose Fasting), conflicting reference intervals, and divergent units ($nmol/L$ vs $ng/mL$).

The 7-Minute Consultation

A consulting physician has approximately 5 to 7 minutes per patient. No doctor has the time to open 15 PDF attachments or spreadsheets to calculate rates of change.

The consequence is devastating: sub-clinical disease velocity is invisible. A patient's fasting blood glucose drifting upward at $+7\text{ mg/dL}$ per year is labeled "Normal" each year because it sits just under the arbitrary $100\text{ mg/dL}$ cutoff—until the year it crosses into pre-diabetes.

Our 4 Engineering Pillars

01. Deterministic Math Over Generative AI

We refuse to use non-deterministic LLMs for calculating units or reading numbers. Conversion relies on exact molecular weight formulas (e.g. Glucose molar mass $180.16\text{ g/mol}$).

02. Confidence-Gated Handwriting Fallback

When OCR confidence for doctor handwriting is under $95\%$, we never hallucinate drug dosages. The system crops and attaches the authentic original handwriting image directly into the report.

03. Relative Range Position ($RRP\%$)

Standardizes values across conflicting laboratory equipment reference intervals to prevent false alarms between different hospital chains.

04. Privacy Safe Harbor & DPDP Compliance

One-click PII masking replaces patient names and contact data with pseudonymous Universal Health IDs (`ChronoID`) for anonymous second opinions.
