Core Engineering

Four Pillars of Clinical Precision

Every design choice in ChronoLab prioritizes mathematical reproducibility, safety, and patient privacy.

⚗️

Deterministic Unit Conversion

Standardizes international units (Vitamin D $nmol/L \leftrightarrow ng/mL$, Glucose $mmol/L \leftrightarrow mg/dL$) using molecular weights ($M_r$).

📐

Relative Range Position ($RRP\%$)

Calculates where observed numbers sit as a percentage within conflicting lab reference bands, eliminating provider equipment disparities.

✍️

Zero-Hallucination Fallback

Confidence-gated prescription processing. If handwriting OCR is < 95%, crops the authentic doctor snippet rather than hallucinating doses.

🛡️

DPDP & HIPAA De-Identification

1-Click PII masking replaces patient identity with pseudonymous Universal Health IDs (`ChronoID`) for anonymous second opinions.
